If you also have debt, this sheet has one more job

Challenge money sitting in savings while a Tala loan, a GCredit draw, a BillEase installment, or a card balance costs you 2 to 15 percent a month loses money every month it sits. Once a small starter buffer exists, the weekly deposit belongs at the top of your payoff order instead.
